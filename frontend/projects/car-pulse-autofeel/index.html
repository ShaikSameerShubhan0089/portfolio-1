Car Pulse (AutoFeel)

A review-analysis application that groups car sentiment by brand and model.

01 · The problem

What this project addresses

Raw car reviews make it difficult to compare how people feel about individual makes and models.

02 · Implementation

How it works

A Flask application cleans submitted reviews with NLTK, classifies sentiment with a scikit-learn model, and groups results by brand and model.

03 · Outcome

What it does

Submitted reviews are classified as positive, negative, or neutral and summarized into brand- and model-level trends.

Technologies

  • Python
  • Flask
  • NLTK
  • scikit-learn
